About

Software-focused long/short equity investing. Polarity applies a disciplined, multi-framework investment process that integrates fundamental analysis, quantitative finance, and deep, thesis-driven research.

We focus on identifying asymmetric opportunities through rigorous analysis, supported by systematic data evaluation and an understanding of market structure inefficiencies. Our approach is inherently cross-disciplinary, integrating industry positioning, and company-specific fundamentals to develop high-conviction views.

Portfolio construction is driven by risk-adjusted return optimisation. We prioritise consistency and capital preservation, targeting strong risk-adjusted performance as measured by metrics rather than pursuing absolute returns in isolation. Position sizing, factor awareness, and diversification across independent return drivers are central to this process.

Risk management is embedded at every stage. We continuously reassess exposures, stress-test assumptions in portfolios, and adapt to evolving market conditions to ensure resilience across cycles and regimes.

Polarity is built to deliver durable, repeatable performance in an increasingly complex and dynamic investment environment.

Our Strategy

Polarity employs a disciplined, multi-framework investment approach combining fundamental analysis, quantitative methods, and deep research to identify mispriced opportunities.

We take a high-conviction, selective approach, allocating capital where risk-reward is asymmetric. Our investment horizon ranges from months to years, allowing us to capture both shorter-term dislocations and longer-term fundamental value.

Portfolio construction is driven by risk-adjusted return optimisation, with a focus on consistency rather than absolute returns alone.

Our edge lies in the integration of multiple analytical disciplines, allowing us to identify opportunities that are often overlooked by single-framework approaches. This is reinforced by a research-driven process, continuous learning, and the ability to adapt quickly as market conditions evolve.

Risk management is embedded throughout the process, with active position sizing, diversification across independent return drivers, and continuous reassessment of exposures to ensure resilience across market conditions.

Our strategy is designed to deliver durable, repeatable performance while maintaining adaptability in an increasingly complex investment environment.
